# Legends Adventure

**2D Top-Down Action RPG | Game Design | Unity | C#**

## Game Overview

Legends Adventure is a 2D top-down action RPG inspired by *Soul Knight* and *The Legend of Zelda*. The game takes players on an adventure across an uncharted island filled with monsters, dangerous environments, hidden paths, and challenging bosses.

The core experience focuses on real-time combat, dungeon exploration, enemy encounters, and character progression. Players must master movement and combat abilities, overcome environmental challenges, and manage resources to progress through the adventure.

## Core Gameplay Loop

**Explore → Encounter Enemies → Combat → Collect Resources → Progress → Face Greater Challenges**

Players explore dungeon environments, engage in combat, collect gold and items, and use resources to improve their chances of surviving increasingly challenging encounters.

## Game Design Highlights

### 1. Combat Design
- Designed real-time melee combat centered around player movement, positioning, and attack timing.
- Incorporated a dash mechanic to support enemy avoidance and combat mobility.
- Developed different enemy attack patterns to create varied combat encounters.
- Designed boss encounters that challenge players to observe enemy behavior and respond accordingly.

### 2. Enemy Design
- Designed enemy behaviors based on detection, movement, and attack patterns.
- Established different combat behaviors to create variety in encounters.
- Implemented enemy AI to support different gameplay challenges.

### 3. Level Design & Exploration
- Structured dungeon exploration through interconnected areas and portals.
- Designed environmental traps to introduce additional challenges.
- Combined enemy encounters and environmental obstacles to create varied gameplay situations.
- Used level layouts to guide player exploration and progression.

### 4. Player Progression & Game Systems
- Designed health and mana systems to manage player survival and abilities.
- Incorporated gold and item collection as resource-based progression.
- Designed a shop system that allows players to use collected resources.
- Connected exploration, combat rewards, and resource management to support the overall progression loop.
